# IdleHold

<!-- Logo goes here -->

**IdleHold** is an idle game for the browser, currently in development.

Pick a skill, choose what to work on, and watch your progress grow, even while you are away. Gather resources, level up your skills and slowly build up your hold over time.

Idle, but not passive: IdleHold is designed so that time spent actively playing actually matters, through choices and moments that reward being there, not just a faster progress bar.

## Status

🚧 Early development. Not playable yet.
